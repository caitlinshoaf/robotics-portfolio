Object redesign challenge

fishing rod + heavy / unmovable = FishTank

This was an object redesign challenge where we were given an object and a format, as groups we then had to merge these and reimagine a new object. My group was given "fishing rod" and "heavy / unmovable". We designed an underwater contraption that shoots metal magnets at fish, then uses electromagnets to collect the fish, the fish are then sent through a vaccuum tube and an underwater conveyor belt to reach land. This contraption can help restaurants increase their incoming source of fish, while reducing the labor necessary for collection.
