Project 11: High-or-Low?

For this project, we used our new knowledge of machine learning and nearest-neighbor algorithms to make a device that would detect high and low heights. I first trained my detector by scanning various heights and classifying them as either "high" or "low" with motor input. Then, in inference mode, my detector was able to use the K-nearest neighbor algorithm to compare new data with the algorithm to decide whether it's high or low.
