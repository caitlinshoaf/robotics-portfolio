Project 12: Fruit Scanner

This project expanded on using Nearest-Neighbor algorithms. By utilizing a color sensor and 3-variable comparison, my device was able to scan a piece of fruit and detect its fruit type. After training the device with data from multiple apples and bananas, it was able to correctly categorize other pieces of fruit as "apple" or "banana".
