Activity 3: Motor Encoding

Today in class we learnt about different sensors and their uses. We were split into teams, with each team tasked to learn about and code for a different kind of sensor. My group was asked with Motor encoding. We used the angle of motor A to control the speed of motor B. When motor A was twisted from 0 degrees to 360 degrees, the speed of motor B would increase proportionally between 0% and 100%. Click here to see our project.
