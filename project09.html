Project 09: Robotic Arm

This week's project was building a robotic arm to transport a ping pong ball. My robotic arm used two motors, one controlling the opening and closing of the claw, the other controlling the rotating of the arm. There is a color sensor that activates the program when a colored ball is placed on the first platform. The arm comes down, and the claw closes around the ball. The ball is then picked up and lifted up and over the central hub. It is finally released on the second platform.
