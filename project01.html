Getting to know you Lego-build

Lego Replica of a Water Buffalo

Our first lego-building project was to help our professor and classmates get to know us. I grew up in a small village in Hong Kong, where water buffaloes roam freely. They were used for plowing rice fields until the 70s and now wander around the village, lay in muddy fields and swim in the rivers. Although Hong Kong is known for its skyscrapers and busy streets, water buffaloes are one thing that truly reminds me of home. I felt like building a water buffalo was a good way to show my unique background. This project was a good way to get familiar with all the pieces in the Lego kit we will be using for the rest of the semester. It was a challenge building something at a smaller scale, and without the motor / gear pieces, but now I'm much more comfortable working with the kit as we move onto more complex projects.
